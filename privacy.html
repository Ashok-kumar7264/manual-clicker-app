Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Plain-language summary: Manual Clicker is an Android utility app. We collect only information reasonably needed to provide the app, process purchases, maintain subscriptions, prevent abuse, and provide support. Payment card/UPI credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by Manual Clicker.

1. Who we are

Manual Clicker (“Manual Clicker”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides an Android application that performs user-configured screen taps through Android Accessibility Service.

Support email: support.manualclicker@gmail.com

2. We Dont collect

  • NO Account or purchase information such as name, email address, mobile number, transaction reference, subscription plan and subscription dates, where required to activate or support a purchase.
  • Device-related information required to identify a device or activate a subscription, such as an Android device identifier, where the app's implementation uses one.
  • Technical information such as app version, device model, Android version, crash/error information and basic diagnostic logs when needed to troubleshoot the service.
  • Information you voluntarily send to support, such as screenshots, messages or payment references.

3. Accessibility Service

Manual Clicker uses Android Accessibility Service to perform the taps and actions configured by the user. The service operates on the device and is enabled by the user through Android settings. Manual Clicker does not claim ownership of information displayed by other apps.

Users should review the privacy policies and terms of any other app they use with Manual Clicker.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and maintain Manual Clicker.
  • Activate and manage paid subscriptions.
  • Process and verify payments through our payment provider.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and misuse.
  • Provide customer support and resolve payment or technical issues.
  • Improve reliability, security and performance.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed by Razorpay or another authorized payment provider. We do not ask customers to send or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, banking passwords or other payment credentials with Manual Clicker. Payment information is handled by the payment provider under its own applicable privacy notices and security practices.

6. Sharing of information

We may share necessary information with service providers that help us operate the app, process payments, host infrastructure, provide analytics or customer support, or comply with law. We do not sell personal information as a business model.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including subscription records, accounting, dispute handling, security and legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your use of Manual Clicker, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask about deletion where deletion is legally and operationally possible. Some transaction or legal records may need to be retained.

9. Children's privacy

Manual Clicker is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for independent purchases or subscriptions.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
