Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

24-hour refund request window: If you believe you were charged incorrectly or the purchased subscription was not activated, contact us within 24 hours of the payment with your payment/order reference. This is the request window; the time taken for the refunded amount to appear in your bank account or payment instrument may depend on the payment provider and bank.

1. Subscription cancellation

Because Manual Clicker subscriptions provide digital access, cancellation stops future use/renewal where applicable but does not automatically create a refund for time or access already provided.

2. When a refund may be approved

  • Payment was successful but the purchased subscription was not activated and we are unable to correct the activation issue.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase because of a duplicate transaction.
  • You were charged for a different plan from the one successfully purchased and the issue cannot reasonably be corrected by adjusting the subscription.
  • A material technical failure on our side prevents delivery of the paid service and we are unable to resolve it within a reasonable period.
  • Another refund is required by applicable law.

3. Situations normally not eligible for refund

  • Change of mind after the service has been activated or used.
  • Failure to use the subscription during its validity period.
  • Problems caused by a third-party application, Android device, manufacturer restriction, network connection or operating-system change.
  • Suspension caused by fraud, subscription bypass, unauthorized sharing, abuse or violation of these Terms.
  • Requests made after the 24-hour refund request window, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

4. How to request a refund

Email [REPLACE WITH YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] with:

  • Your name and registered mobile/email, if applicable.
  • Payment/order ID or Razorpay payment reference.
  • Purchase date and plan.
  • A short explanation of the issue.

Do not send your UPI PIN, CVV, card PIN, banking password or full card details.

5. Refund processing

Once a refund is approved, we will initiate it through the payment method/payment provider used for the transaction. We will communicate the refund status or reference where available. The final credit time can depend on Razorpay, the relevant bank, card network, UPI system or other payment provider.

6. Failed or reversed payments

If your account was debited but the payment was not successfully completed or the subscription was not activated, first allow the payment provider's normal reconciliation process to complete. If the amount remains unresolved, contact us with the payment reference.

7. Policy changes

We may update this policy when our plans or payment processes change. The version published on this page applies to purchases made after the effective date, subject to applicable law.
